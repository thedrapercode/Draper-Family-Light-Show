Our lovely house lights up and puts on a show! From 6pm until 11pm you can catch our FREE Draper Family Light and Projection show.

Our lights and projections are choreographed along to music and you can hear the audio by tuning your radio to 106.9FM.

Our Light show is made for the whole family to enjoy and our LOVE show is no different! See you at our Lights this year.

**Become a part of our Light Show:** enter your name and your loved one's name (and even a photo) to be featured during segments of the show. Submissions aren't guaranteed to be used, but we do our best. Deadline for entry is January 31st.

We are proud to support the United Way of Hardin County.
