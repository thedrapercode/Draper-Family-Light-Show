Our house lights up in honor of Independence Day! From 6pm until 11pm you can catch our FREE Draper Family Light and Projection show.

Our house lights are choreographed along to music and can be heard by tuning your car FM Radio to 106.9 FM.

Draper Family LibertyLights are free and completely safe for kids of every age.

We are proud to support the United Way of Hardin County.
