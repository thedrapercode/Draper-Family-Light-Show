Our Christmas house lights up and puts on a show! From 6pm until 11pm you can catch our FREE Draper Family Light show in Kenton.

Our house lights are choreographed along to music and can be heard by tuning your car FM Radio to 106.9 FM.

Draper Family Christmas Lights are fun, free, and completely safe for kids of every age.

Proud to support the United Way of Hardin County with our bright Christmas.
