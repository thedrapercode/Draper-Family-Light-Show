Our House, not so far away, lights up and puts on a show! At 8pm you can catch our FREE Draper Family Light and Projection show with your whole family.

Our lights and projections are choreographed to music from Star Wars, played live on site through our speaker system, by tuning your radio to 106.9FM in your car, or using our website audio streaming.

Our Light show is made for the whole family to enjoy and our Star Wars May the 4th Be With You show is no different! See you at our Lights this year. Dress up and have your phones ready for a great photo opportunity. Everything is Star Wars related.

### FAQ

**What is a light show?** Our house light show features individually controllable LED lights (pixels) that allow for precise color changes and animations, synchronized to music or video.

**Is the show really free?** Yes, you can visit us on May 4th and watch the show without paying for anything. We do ask that you support United Way of Hardin County if you're able, since 98% of donations stay within Hardin County.
